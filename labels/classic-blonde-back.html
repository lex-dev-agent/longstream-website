Longstream Distillery

Small-batch dry gin from the Mangaroa Valley. Initially crisp and piney, quickly followed by warm eucalyptus, finished with a refreshing citrus note. Copper distilled using pure Te Awa Kairangi water. Best served with a simple tonic.
